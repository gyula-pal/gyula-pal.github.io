Privacy policy

Applies to "rclone homelab", a personal backup tool. Last updated 15 November 2026.

Who uses it

Only its owner, with his own Google account. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

Files in the owner's own Google Drive, which it copies to and from the owner's own computers.

What happens to the data

Google data is stored only on the owner's own computers. Nothing is collected from anyone else, sold, or shared with third parties.
